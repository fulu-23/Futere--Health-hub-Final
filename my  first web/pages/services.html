NURSING

It is a healthcare profession focused on caring for sick,injured and recovering patients. nurses work in hospitals,clinics and communites to monitor patients,gives medication,assist doctors and support people physical and emotional needs.They play an important role in improving health and preventing illness.

PHARMACIST

Is a healthy professional who prepares ,checks,and give out medicine and help people to use them safely. They work in places like pharmacies,hospitals and clinics.When a doctor prescribes medicine the pharmacist makes sure it is the right one to become a pharmacist you usually study pharmacy at university learn about drugs,chemistry and the human body.

SURGEON

Is a medical doctor who specializes in perfoming operation to treat injuries ,diseases or other health problems.They work in hospitals amd use advanced medical tools and techniques to safely operate on patient and improve or save lives.surgeons work in hospitals and often collaborate with other healthcare proffesional to ensure patient receive the best care before ,during and after surgery.To become surgeon you must study medicine at university
