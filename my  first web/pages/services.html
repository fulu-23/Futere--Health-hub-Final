NURSING

Nursing is a healthcare profession that focuses on caring for patients, helping them recover from illness, and supporting doctors in treatment.

What nurses do:

They check patients’ vital signs and monitor health conditions.

They give medication as prescribed by doctors.

They assist patients with daily care and hygiene.

They support patients and provide emotional care.

Where nurses work:

Hospitals

Clinics

Old age homes

Community health centres

Requirements to become a nurse:

You must have a Grade 12 certificate (Matric).

You must pass subjects like Mathematics, Life Sciences, and English.

You must study Nursing at a college or university.

You must complete practical training in a hospital or clinic.

You must be caring, patient, and able to work under pressure.

PHARMACIST

A pharmacist is a healthcare professional who prepares and gives medicine to patients. They also explain how to use medicine safely and correctly.

Duties of a Pharmacist

  • Dispense medicine to patients
  • Explain how medication should be used
  • Advise patients about side effects
  • Check prescriptions from doctors
  • Keep patient medicine records

Skills Needed

  • Good communication skills
  • Problem-solving skills
  • Attention to detail
  • Knowledge of medicine
  • Ability to work with people

Places Where Pharmacists Work

  • Hospitals
  • Clinics
  • Community pharmacies
  • Pharmaceutical companies

Importance of Pharmacists

Pharmacists help people use medicine safely and correctly. They play an important role in improving healthcare.

SURGEON

A surgeon is a medical doctor who performs operations to treat injuries, diseases, and other health problems. Surgeons use special medical equipment during surgery to help patients recover and improve their health.

Duties of a Surgeon

  • Perform surgical operations on patients
  • Examine patients before surgery
  • Diagnose medical conditions
  • Work with nurses and other doctors
  • Monitor patients after surgery

Skills Needed

  • Good communication skills
  • Problem-solving skills
  • Attention to detail
  • Ability to work under pressure
  • Knowledge of medicine and surgery

Places Where Surgeons Work

  • Hospitals
  • Medical clinics
  • Private healthcare centres
  • Universities and research institutions

Importance of Surgeons

Surgeons help save lives by treating serious injuries and diseases through medical operations. They play an important role in healthcare and patient recovery.

DENTIST

A dentist is a healthcare professional who treats problems related to teeth, gums, and the mouth. Dentists help patients maintain healthy teeth and improve oral hygiene.

Duties of a Dentist

  • Examine patients' teeth and gums
  • Clean and remove cavities from teeth
  • Fill damaged teeth
  • Teach patients about oral hygiene
  • Perform dental procedures and treatments

Skills Needed

  • Good communication skills
  • Attention to detail
  • Problem-solving skills
  • Patience and care for patients
  • Knowledge of dental care

Places Where Dentists Work

  • Dental clinics
  • Hospitals
  • Private practices
  • Universities and research centres

Importance of Dentists

Dentists help people maintain healthy teeth and gums. They play an important role in preventing and treating oral diseases.

PHYSIOTHERAPHY

A physiotherapist is a healthcare professional who helps patients improve movement and reduce pain after injuries, illnesses, or disabilities. Physiotherapists use exercises and physical treatment to help patients recover.

Duties of a Physiotherapist

  • Help patients recover from injuries
  • Teach patients physical exercises
  • Reduce pain and improve movement
  • Monitor patient progress
  • Work with doctors and other healthcare professionals

Skills Needed

  • Good communication skills
  • Patience and care for patients
  • Problem-solving skills
  • Knowledge of physical therapy
  • Ability to motivate patients

Places Where Physiotherapists Work

  • Hospitals
  • Rehabilitation centres
  • Sports clinics
  • Private healthcare centres

Importance of Physiotherapists

Physiotherapists help patients recover from injuries and improve their quality of life. They play an important role in rehabilitation and physical health.
